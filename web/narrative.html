What is FSNI?

FSNI measures the intensity and spread of negative narrative events affecting the food sector. It combines two axes — Intensity (how severe the coverage is) and Diffusion (how widely it is spreading). When elevated, it signals sector-level contagion risk — not a single company crisis, but a narrative wave capable of affecting the whole industry's revenue.

Contracts settle against this index. A YES contract pays out if the index mean exceeds the threshold during the contract window. You are not predicting whether a food safety event happens — you are taking a position on whether the narrative around it spreads.
